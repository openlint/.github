# OpenLint

**An open specification and toolbox for linting API artifacts**: rulesets, conformance, and governance for OpenAPI and the formats around it. A community fork of Spectral, developed and stewarded in the open as an [API Commons](https://apicommons.org) effort.

The name was chosen by [open community vote](https://github.com/api-commons/spotlight-spec/issues/39) in September 2026, with [public due diligence](https://github.com/api-commons/spotlight-spec/issues/45). Everything, from the specification to the tooling and the governance, happens in public, here.

## What is being built

Three things, deliberately decoupled so no one of them belongs to a single tool or vendor:

1. **The specification**: a normative format for rulesets that a government can cite in a procurement document and a tool can claim conformance with.
2. **The toolbox**: the linter as a library, a programmatic API, a CLI, a CI gate, and an IDE integration.
3. **The community**: an open home, open governance, and an Open Collective to fund the work.

## Get involved

Pick up something [unclaimed on the roadmap](https://openlint.org/roadmap/), start a [discussion](https://github.com/orgs/openlint/discussions), or come to [office hours](https://openlint.org/meetings/). To be added to the invite, ask in the [maintainers discussion](https://github.com/orgs/openlint/discussions/19) or email [info@openlint.org](mailto:info@openlint.org). If you run Spectral rulesets today, in an enterprise, a government or a standards body, your requirements are exactly what the roadmap needs.
